Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you use this website or enroll in a program with us.

1. Information we collect

When you submit our enrollment form, call, email, or visit us in person, you may give us information such as your name, phone number, email address, program of interest, preferred start date, and any message you include.

If you enroll in a program, our admissions and instructional staff may collect additional information required for your certification, such as proof of identity, background check results, immunization or health screening records, and attendance and completion records. This information is collected directly as part of enrollment, not through this website, and is used only to administer your enrollment and meet state and certifying body requirements.

Like most websites, our web server automatically records standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, for security and basic site administration. This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts; loading these fonts may share standard request information, such as your IP address, with Google under Google's own privacy policy. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

To keep our enrollment form free of automated spam, submissions pass through anti-spam checks that include a short human-verification question, hidden trap fields, and a short-term, hashed record of your connection's IP address used for rate limiting. These records are used only for security, are not linked to your enrollment file, and expire automatically within one hour.

2. How we use your information

  • Respond to enrollment inquiries and questions submitted through this website
  • Contact you about course availability, schedule, and enrollment requirements
  • Administer your enrollment and track required certification documentation if you become a student
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of this website
  • Meet legal, regulatory, and accreditation requirements that apply to our programs

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share enrollment and certification information with the California CNA registry, the American Heart Association, or other certifying bodies, as required to process your certification. We may also share information with service providers who help us operate this website or our school, such as our hosting provider, who are required to protect your information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Nura Care Institute, our students, or others.

4. Data retention

We keep enrollment form submissions and related communications for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, or, if you enroll, for as long as required to maintain your student and certification records under applicable state requirements.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's privacy

Our programs and this website are intended for adults seeking healthcare certification training. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your choices and rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, request a correction, or request that we delete information you submitted through this website. Some information related to an active or completed certification may need to be retained to comply with state or certifying body requirements. To make a request, email education@nuracareinstitute.com or call (916) 544-1256.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
