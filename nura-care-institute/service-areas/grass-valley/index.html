Grass Valley, Nevada County

Mobile CPR and BLS Training in Grass Valley, California

We bring American Heart Association BLS and ACLS training up to Grass Valley and Nevada County, delivered onsite so foothill teams stay current close to home.

Book a Class Call (916) 544-1256

Nura Care Institute offers mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training in Grass Valley and the surrounding Nevada County foothills. Rather than asking your staff to drive down the hill to Sacramento, we travel up to you and run the complete hands-on session at your facility.

Grass Valley and neighboring Nevada City anchor a foothill community with clinics, senior care homes, dental offices, and a strong network of small employers. For these teams, onsite training removes a long mountain drive and keeps everyone certified together.

Grass Valley is about a 50 minute drive northeast of our Orangevale campus by way of Highway 49.

Who we train in Grass Valley

Local teams we certify

Grass Valley is served by community health clinics, assisted living and skilled nursing homes, dental and therapy practices, and small businesses, all of which need staff with a current BLS or CPR card. We bring renewal and new-hire certification directly to them.

We also serve the communities around Grass Valley, including Nevada City, Penn Valley, Lake of the Pines, Rough and Ready, and Auburn.

How mobile training works
  1. Request a date. Tell us your location, group size, and the course you need.
  2. We travel to you. Your instructor brings the manikins, AED trainers, and materials to your Grass Valley site.
  3. Your team gets certified. Everyone completes the hands-on session together, on your schedule.
