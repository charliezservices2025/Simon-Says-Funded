Auburn, Placer County

Mobile CPR and BLS Training in Auburn, California

We bring American Heart Association BLS and ACLS training to Auburn and the surrounding Gold Country, delivered onsite to your team or facility.

Book a Class Call (916) 544-1256

Nura Care Institute provides mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training in Auburn and the foothill communities of eastern Placer County. We travel up from the valley, set up at your location, and run the full hands-on certification session so your staff never have to drive down to Sacramento.

Auburn sits in the Sierra foothills along Interstate 80, where clinics, senior care homes, outdoor and recreation employers, and small medical practices all need staff with current life support cards. Onsite training saves foothill teams the long round trip to a valley classroom.

Auburn is roughly a 30 minute drive northeast of our Orangevale campus on Interstate 80.

Who we train in Auburn

Local teams we certify

Auburn and the surrounding Gold Country are served by community clinics, dental offices, assisted living and memory care homes, dentists, and recreation and outdoor programs. Each one needs current BLS or CPR certification, and we bring that training to their door.

We also serve the communities around Auburn, including Newcastle, Meadow Vista, Colfax, Foresthill, Loomis, and Grass Valley.

How mobile training works
  1. Request a date. Tell us your location, group size, and the course you need.
  2. We travel to you. Your instructor brings the manikins, AED trainers, and materials to your Auburn site.
  3. Your team gets certified. Everyone completes the hands-on session together, on your schedule.

All BLS and ACLS instruction is aligned with current American Heart Association guidelines and taught in person by an experienced instructor.
