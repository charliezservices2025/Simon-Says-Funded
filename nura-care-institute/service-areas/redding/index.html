Redding, Shasta County

Mobile CPR and BLS Training in Redding, California

We bring American Heart Association BLS and ACLS training north to Redding and Shasta County, delivered onsite to your team or facility.

Book a Class Call (916) 544-1256

Nura Care Institute provides mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training in Redding and across Shasta County. As a mobile training program, we travel up Interstate 5 to your location, bring the equipment, and run the full hands-on session so your far-north team certifies without traveling to the valley.

Redding anchors California's far north, with a regional medical community, clinics, senior care facilities, and outdoor and recreation employers that all need current life support certification. Onsite group training spares these teams a long drive and keeps everyone certified together.

Redding is roughly a 2.5 hour drive north of our Orangevale campus up Interstate 5, so far-north sessions are arranged as scheduled visits.

Who we train in Redding

Local teams we certify

Redding and Shasta County are served by hospitals and clinics, skilled nursing and assisted living homes, dental offices, schools, and outdoor recreation employers. Each needs staff with a current BLS or CPR card, and we bring that training onsite throughout the area.

We also serve the communities around Redding, including Anderson, Shasta Lake, Cottonwood, Red Bluff, and Palo Cedro.

How mobile training works
  1. Request a date. Let us know where you are in Shasta County, how many people need training, and which course fits.
  2. We drive north to you. Your instructor loads up the manikins, AED trainers, and course materials and heads to your Redding location.
  3. Your team gets certified. Everybody works through the hands-on skills together in a single visit, at a time that suits your shifts.

Whether you book BLS or ACLS, every class follows the current American Heart Association guidelines and is led face to face by an experienced instructor who makes the trip up to Redding.
