Mobile CPR and BLS Training in Yuba City, California

We bring American Heart Association BLS and ACLS training to Yuba City and the Yuba-Sutter area, delivered onsite to your team or facility.

Book a Class Call (916) 544-1256

Nura Care Institute provides mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training across Yuba City, Marysville, and the wider Yuba-Sutter area. We travel north to your location, bring the equipment, and run the full hands-on session so your staff certify without a long drive south.

The Yuba-Sutter region is served by hospitals, clinics, agricultural employers, schools, and senior care facilities, many of which have teams that need a current life support card. Onsite training keeps these groups certified together and on schedule.

Yuba City is roughly a 45 minute drive north of our Orangevale campus by way of Highway 70.

Who we train in Yuba City

Local teams we certify

Across Yuba City and Marysville, medical and dental offices, skilled nursing and assisted living facilities, schools, and workplace safety teams all need current BLS or CPR certification. We deliver renewals and new-hire training onsite throughout the Yuba-Sutter area.

  1. Request a date. Tell us your location, group size, and the course you need.
