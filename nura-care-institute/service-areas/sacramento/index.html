Mobile CPR and BLS Training in Sacramento, California

We bring American Heart Association BLS and ACLS training directly to teams, clinics, and facilities throughout the Sacramento area, so your staff get certified without leaving the building.

Book a Class Call (916) 544-1256

Nura Care Institute provides mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training across Sacramento, the state capital and the hub of our service region. Instead of sending your team across town to a classroom, we come to you, set up in your conference room or break room, and run the full hands-on session onsite.

Because our home campus sits just northeast of the city in Orangevale, Sacramento is one of the closest and most frequently scheduled areas we serve. That means short lead times, flexible dates, and easy coordination for offices, care homes, dental practices, gyms, and community groups that need a current certification card.

Sacramento is roughly a 20 minute drive from our Orangevale campus, so we can often schedule sessions on short notice.

Who we train in Sacramento

Local teams we certify

Sacramento is home to hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, outpatient clinics, dental and veterinary offices, fitness studios, schools, and county programs, and every one of them has staff who need a current BLS or CPR card. We train new hires, whole departments preparing for an audit, and individual professionals who simply need to renew before their card expires.

We also serve the communities around Sacramento, including Elk Grove, Rancho Cordova, Citrus Heights, Arden-Arcade, West Sacramento, and Davis.

How mobile training works
  1. Request a date. Tell us your location, group size, and the course you need.
  2. We travel to you. Your instructor brings the manikins, AED trainers, and materials to your Sacramento site.
  3. Your team gets certified. Everyone completes the hands-on session together, on your schedule.
