Napa, Napa County

Mobile CPR and BLS Training in Napa, California

We bring American Heart Association BLS and ACLS training to Napa and the surrounding wine country, delivered onsite to your team or facility.

Book a Class Call (916) 544-1256

Nura Care Institute offers mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training in Napa and across Napa County. We travel into wine country, set up at your location, and run the complete hands-on certification session so your team certifies on site.

Napa supports a busy hospitality and healthcare economy, from wineries and resorts to clinics, spas, and senior living communities. Many of these employers keep first responders and trained staff on hand, and onsite group training is the most efficient way to keep those certifications current.

Napa is roughly a 90 minute drive southwest of our Orangevale campus, so wine country sessions are scheduled as planned visits.

Who we train in Napa

Local teams we certify

Napa employers include wineries and resorts, hotels and restaurants, day spas, medical and dental offices, and senior care communities, all of which benefit from staff who hold a current CPR or BLS card. We bring onsite certification to hospitality and healthcare teams alike.

We also serve the communities around Napa, including American Canyon, Yountville, St. Helena, Vallejo, and Fairfield.

How mobile training works
  1. Request a date. Tell us your location, group size, and the course you need.
  2. We travel to you. Your instructor brings the manikins, AED trainers, and materials to your Napa site.
  3. Your team gets certified. Everyone completes the hands-on session together, on your schedule.

All BLS and ACLS instruction is aligned with current American Heart Association guidelines and taught in person by an experienced instructor.
