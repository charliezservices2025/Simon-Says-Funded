Nura Care Institute offers mobile CPR and Basic Life Support training throughout Roseville and the surrounding Placer County communities. We travel to your office, clinic, or facility, bring the manikins and AED trainers, and run the complete hands-on session on site.

Roseville sits just up Interstate 80 from our Orangevale campus, which makes it one of the easiest areas for us to reach. Growing medical offices, dental practices, senior living communities, and corporate teams across the city rely on onsite training to keep their certifications current without losing a half day to travel.

How mobile training works
  1. Request a date. Tell us your location, group size, and the course you need.
  2. We travel to you. Your instructor brings the manikins, AED trainers, and materials to your Roseville site.
  3. Your team gets certified. Everyone completes the hands-on session together, on your schedule.

All BLS and ACLS instruction is aligned with current American Heart Association guidelines and taught in person by an experienced instructor.
