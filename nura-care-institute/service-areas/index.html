Why mobile training

Instead of sending your whole team across the region to a classroom, we bring the full hands-on class to your door.

  • Onsite group sessions at your workplace, clinic, or facility
  • American Heart Association aligned BLS and ACLS courses
  • One visit certifies your whole team together
  • Flexible dates that work around your schedule
