ACLS-100

AHA Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS)

Advanced training in recognizing and managing cardiac arrest, stroke, and acute coronary emergencies as part of a clinical response team.

$210per course

Reviewed by Tynesha Zacarias, Lead Instructor. Last updated July 2, 2026.

What this course covers

ACLS builds on basic life support skills with advanced airway management, recognition of cardiac arrest and peri-arrest rhythms, pharmacology used during resuscitation, and team-based megacode scenarios. Instruction emphasizes clear roles and communication during a real resuscitation event, not just individual skills in isolation.

Who this is for

ACLS is designed for nurses, paramedics, and other clinical providers who may respond to cardiac emergencies as part of their role. It is a recommended next step after Basic Life Support for anyone moving into more acute or critical care responsibilities.

Prerequisites

A current AHA BLS Provider certification is typically recommended before starting ACLS, along with a working knowledge of basic cardiac rhythms. If you are not sure whether you meet the prerequisites, call our admissions team and we will help you confirm before you enroll.
