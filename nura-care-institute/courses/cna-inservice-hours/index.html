INSV-12

CNA In-Service Hours (12-Hour Session)

A 12-hour in-service training session for active Certified Nursing Assistants, counting toward the continuing education hours you need to keep your certification current.

$30012 hours

What this session covers

This in-service session refreshes core CNA competencies and covers current best practices in patient care, infection control, safety, and documentation. Content is structured to count toward the in-service hours California requires active CNAs to complete to keep their certification current.

Who this is for

This session is for currently certified CNAs who need in-service hours toward renewal. If you are not sure how many hours you still need, bring your renewal notice or registry information and our team will help you confirm.

Confirm your renewal requirement

In-service hour requirements and renewal timelines are set by the state, so we recommend confirming your exact renewal date and hours owed directly with the California CNA registry alongside booking your session with us.

Looking to specialize further instead? Active CNAs can also pursue our Restorative Nursing Assistant course.
