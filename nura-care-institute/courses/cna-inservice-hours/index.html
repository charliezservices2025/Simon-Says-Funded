CEU-6

CNA Continuing Education (6 CEUs)

A six CEU continuing education session for active Certified Nursing Assistants, counting toward the in-service hours you need to keep your California certification current.

$506 CEUs

What this session covers

This continuing education session delivers 6 CEUs and refreshes core CNA competencies, covering current best practices in patient care, infection control, safety, and documentation. The hours are structured to count toward the in-service training California requires active CNAs to complete to keep their certification current.

Who this is for

This session is for currently certified CNAs who need continuing education or in-service hours toward renewal. If you are not sure how many hours you still need, bring your renewal notice or registry information and our team will help you confirm.

Confirm your renewal requirement

Continuing education and in-service hour requirements and renewal timelines are set by the state, so we recommend confirming your exact renewal date and hours owed directly with the California CNA registry alongside booking your session with us.

Looking to specialize further instead? Active CNAs can also pursue our Restorative Nursing Assistant course.
