HHA-100

Home Health Aide Training

Advanced, hands-on training that prepares active Certified Nursing Assistants to care for clients in their own homes: personal care, mobility, nutrition, and safety.

$575full program

Reviewed by Tynesha Zacarias, Lead Instructor. Last updated July 2, 2026.

What this program covers

Our Home Health Aide program builds on your CNA training with the day-to-day skills of in-home caregiving: bathing and grooming assistance, safe transfers and mobility support, meal preparation and feeding assistance, light housekeeping related to client care, basic vital signs, and recognizing when a client needs a nurse or emergency response. Instruction is hands-on and practical, built around the real situations aides encounter in a client's home rather than a facility.

Who this is for

This program is for active Certified Nursing Assistants who want to add in-home care to their scope of work. Caring for a client at home requires more independent judgment than working alongside a facility team, which is why we require the clinical foundation of CNA certification and current life support training before you enroll.

Prerequisites

You must hold an active California CNA certification and a current BLS (Basic Life Support) card before enrolling. If you earned either credential elsewhere, bring proof of both when you register. Need the BLS card first? You can complete our AHA BLS course before your HHA start date.

What you'll be able to do

  • Assist with bathing, dressing, and personal hygiene
  • Support safe mobility, transfers, and fall prevention
  • Prepare meals and assist with feeding and nutrition
  • Take and record basic vital signs
  • Recognize warning signs that require a nurse or emergency response
Not a CNA yet? Start with our Certified Nursing Assistant program, add your BLS card, and then return for Home Health Aide training.
