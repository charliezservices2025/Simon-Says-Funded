About the institute

Learn. Care. Empower.

Nura Care Institute trains the next generation of caregivers in Orangevale, California, one small class at a time.

Our approach

Healthcare training works best when students get real practice, not just lecture time. Nura Care Institute keeps classes small so every student gets direct instructor attention while building the hands-on skills caregiving actually requires: taking vital signs, assisting with mobility, responding to emergencies, and documenting care accurately.

We built our programs around one clear pathway. Start with Certified Nursing Assistant training, add your AHA Basic Life Support card, and then specialize: Home Health Aide for in-home care, Restorative Nursing Assistant for rehabilitation support, and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support as your clinical role grows. Active CNAs can also complete their continuing education (CEU) hours with us.

Our campus

Our campus is located at 9198 Greenback Lane, Suite 108, in Orangevale, California, convenient to Citrus Heights, Fair Oaks, Folsom, and the greater Sacramento area.

How we keep this information accurate

Program pages on this site are written and reviewed by our lead instructor, list prerequisites and pricing plainly, and link to official sources such as the California Department of Public Health and the American Heart Association instead of restating rules from memory. Certification requirements are set by the state and by certifying bodies, so we encourage students to confirm details that affect their certification directly with those agencies, and we will help you do it.

Tynesha Zacarias

Tynesha Zacarias leads instruction at Nura Care Institute, bringing hands-on clinical experience and a genuine commitment to helping students build lasting healthcare careers. She works directly with students through classroom instruction and clinical skills practice, from their first day through certification.
